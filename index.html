Our Manifesto

Three Demands.
Non-Negotiable.

This movement is not anti-women. It is pro-child. Every child deserves the love, presence, and protection of both parents — not as a privilege granted by courts, but as a right embedded in law.

India's family law infrastructure was built in 1890. It has not kept pace with a world that recognises shared parenting, equal custody, and the devastating psychological harm of parental alienation. We demand change. Now. Without compromise.

01
Default Co-Parenting from Day 1

Equal parenting time must be default from petition filing unless criminal proof of risk to child exists.

02
Zero Blocking of Child Access

Parental alienation is child abuse. Blocking court-ordered access must carry immediate legal consequences.

03
Criminal Penalty for False Cases

Filing false matrimonial/custody cases must be a cognizable criminal offense with minimum 2-year imprisonment.

India vs The World

Why Is India Decades Behind?

🇮🇳 INDIA — Current Reality 🌍 WORLD STANDARD
Father must prove fitness to the court before receiving any access Both parents are presumed equally fit until proven otherwise
No guaranteed interim access — father may see children months or years later Court-ordered co-parenting begins within 7 days of petition filing
Filing a false matrimonial case carries no criminal penalty Filing false custody/matrimonial cases is a criminal offense
Contested custody battles average 5–10 years in family courts Mandatory 60–90 day mediation with structured resolution timelines
Parental alienation is not legally recognised in Indian family law Parental alienation is legally actionable — courts intervene and enforce
